# English homepage visual refactor

## Direction

Reframe the English homepage as a contemporary technology-community site rather than an editorial report. The resume-workshop page is the visual reference: a floating translucent header, an airy blue gradient canvas, crisp sans-serif hierarchy, generous rounded surfaces, and controlled depth. The Newsreader italic remains only as the distinctive treatment for “shared advantage.”

## Page system

- Use a workshop-derived palette: mist blue canvas, white glass surfaces, clear cyan accents, and deep navy anchors.
- Replace the full-width paper hero with a contained rounded first-view panel and a compact community-pulse visualization.
- Keep the metrics grid and dark background, but replace “Trust at scale” with direct language about community attention and organic reach.
- Simplify the give-forward and shared-path sections into compact, legible systems instead of decorative editorial compositions.
- Rebuild the listening section around the useful insight: roughly 200K conversations reveal needs beyond a CV.
- Rebuild the channel section as a professional community directory with prominent Telegram and YouTube entry points.
- Replace the abstract closing copy with a clear invitation to choose a useful starting point.
- Reduce the footer to brand, social access, “Powered by community,” and the current reporting period.

## Content and behavior

- Preserve verified figures and clearly label cross-platform reach as approximate and non-unique.
- Derive the displayed reporting month from the runtime date instead of hard-coding June 2026.
- Keep English/Persian routing unchanged and use a normal anchor for the language switch.
- Retain restrained reveal and ambient route motion with a complete reduced-motion fallback.

## Verification

- Unit-test reporting-period formatting and the revised content constraints.
- Verify responsive layouts at short mobile, standard mobile, tablet, and desktop sizes.
- Check overflow, first-view CTA visibility, keyboard focus, contrast, reduced motion, Persian isolation, TypeScript, lint, and production build output.
